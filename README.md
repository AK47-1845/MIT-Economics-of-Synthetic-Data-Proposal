# The Economics of Synthetic Data (MIT)

Research proposal with Prof. Catherine Tucker (MIT Sloan), Rozhina (PhD),
Albert Scerbo (MIT Analytics Lab), and Karthikeya Adari (Genuity IO).

## Thesis

The binding friction in healthcare/fintech AI adoption is privacy and
data-sharing regulation (GDPR, HIPAA), not algorithms. Synthetic data decouples
data utility from data identity.

## Research questions

1. Does synthetic data lower the barrier to entry for model training?
2. How do privacy guarantees reshape B2B data monetization strategies?
